# Fourth Atlas pattern banks

Fourth Atlas supplies 12,000 patterns in twelve banks of 1,000, plus a
120-pattern starter bank. They are embedded in NSeqArpKeys 1.4.0. Open
**Pattern Bank** and choose a built-in bank from the bank selector. These
source `.nseqbank` files are kept here for validation and rebuilding the
embedded data.

The six families are Filigree, Antiphon, Embers, Kaleidoscope, Lattice, and
Orbit. Each has a melodic and a rhythmic bank. `catalog.csv` lists the patterns;
`summary.json` gives the generation counts and family statistics.

All Fourth Atlas patterns store `transpose="0"`, so the 1.4.0 change to
Forte-set degree transposition leaves their pitches unchanged. The bank XML
format remains version 1 and can be imported directly in 1.4.0.
